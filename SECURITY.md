# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue, discussion, or pull request for security vulnerabilities.**

Instead, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting:

**[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/ULTRASAT-ORG/Marshal-Issues/security/advisories/new)**

This opens a private advisory visible only to you and the Marshal maintainers. All discussion, triage, and coordination of the fix happens there. A GitHub account is required.

### What to include

- Description of the vulnerability and its impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- Affected Marshal version(s), operating system, and configuration
- Any mitigations you are aware of
- Whether the issue has been disclosed anywhere else

### What to expect

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement of your report | within 5 business days |
| Initial assessment and severity rating | within 10 business days |
| Status updates | at least every 14 days until resolved |
| Fix and coordinated disclosure | agreed with you, typically within 90 days |

If we determine the report is not a security issue, we will explain why and may ask you to open a regular bug report instead.

## Supported versions

Security fixes are provided for the latest released version of Marshal. Older versions may receive fixes at our discretion for critical issues.

| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| Latest release | ✅ |
| Previous releases | ❌ — please upgrade |

## Responsible disclosure

We ask that you:

- Give us a reasonable amount of time to investigate and fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.
- Avoid accessing, modifying, or deleting data that is not your own, and avoid degrading service for other users while testing.
- Do not use social engineering, physical attacks, or denial-of-service against ULTRASAT systems or personnel.

In return, we will:

- Respond promptly and keep you informed.
- Not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith and follow this policy.
- Credit you in the release notes for the fix, if you wish.

## Scope

This policy covers the Marshal application itself. Issues in third-party dependencies should be reported to the respective upstream project; you are welcome to let us know as well so we can track the update.
